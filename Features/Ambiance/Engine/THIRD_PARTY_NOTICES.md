# Audio credits and distribution

The MIT license covers Onde's code, not every third-party recording.
No recordings from commercial streaming catalogs are included.

## Original synthesized sounds — CC0 1.0

Aube, Piano de lune, Orbite, Pluie douce, Marée, Velours brun, Air rose, and
the meditation glass chime are rendered by `Tools/Synthesize.swift` without
external samples. The generated recordings are dedicated under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The nature-like textures are synthesized, not field recordings.
The names Piano de lune and Orbite describe original synthesized compositions,
not recordings of an acoustic piano or binaural/entrainment protocols.

The downloaded MP3 files are unmodified. Their playback volume can be changed
and they may be mixed at playback time. The app displays attribution and a
license link in its Credits view. The build records SHA256 and source URLs in
music-provenance.json and preserves the composer's license FAQ when available.
See https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html

The source archive does not need to contain these MP3s: the build script can
fetch them from the official site. Keep these credits with any binary that
includes them. Availability is not guaranteed; original synthesis still works
when downloads are disabled with ONDE_SKIP_DOWNLOADS=1.

## User imports

Imported files live under ~/Library/Application Support/Onde/Imports, not in
the source tree. They are marked “Private — not redistributed”. Import is not
a copyright license. Users are responsible for having the necessary rights.
The app never automatically exports or uploads imported sound recordings.

## Frameworks and branding

SwiftUI, AppKit, AVFoundation, IOKit and SF Symbols are Apple system resources;
no Apple fonts, symbol files or framework binaries are redistributed as assets.
Onde is an independent project. No equivalence of therapeutic,
cognitive or sleep effects has been established.


## Onde Living Engine — original procedural synthesis

Prisme, Dérive and Immersion are generated by Sources/OndeDSP/OndeDSP.c.
Code MIT; original rendered audio dedicated under CC0 1.0. The synthesis-only profiles embed no third-party recordings, transcriptions or proprietary sound engine.
The runtime uses Apple's installed AVFoundation/Core Audio frameworks.
Personal reference imports remain outside the source and app bundle.

## Living III original profiles (1.4)

Ancrage, Abysses, Courant, Velours, Rive and Immersion use the original
procedural engine in Sources/OndeDSP. Code MIT; newly generated recordings
CC0-1.0. No commercial streaming sample, recording, melody transcription or model weights
are present. Living III disables Living II's synthetic-noise and granular
layers. Personal imports remain outside the distributed app and source.

## Onde 1.6 acoustic orchestra — VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0)

The four orchestral profiles use 67 acoustic note recordings from VSCO 2 CE,
by Versilian Studios / Sam Gossner and the project's contributors.
Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
Pinned commit: 440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e.
License: CC0 1.0; original legal text in Resources/VSCO2-CC0.txt and bundled
Orchestra/LICENSE.txt. Source URLs, MIDI mapping, original and processed
checksums are in Resources/OrchestraSources.json and Orchestra/manifest.json.
Changes: trimmed recording edges, removed DC, controlled normalization,
mild low-pass smoothing, stereo PCM16 conversion and edge fades.
No commercial recordings or personal imports are used by these profiles.
The score and native sampler are original MIT code. The previous procedural
profiles remain available; their synthesis-only descriptions refer to those
profiles, not to the new acoustic sample bank.

## Onde 1.7 acoustic piano and original vowel synthesis

Nine soft upright-piano recordings are included from VSCO 2 Community Edition,
recorded by Simon Dalzell / Ivy Audio and distributed by Versilian Studios.
Their exact mapping, source commit and checksums are in OrchestraSources.json.
The existing VSCO 2 CE CC0 license applies; no proprietary piano plugin is used.

Sanctuaire's ah/oh/oo-like sounds are original additive/formant synthesis in
VowelChoir.c. No recording of a person, external vocal bank or trained model
is used. This is a synthesized vowel ensemble, not an acoustic choir recording.

## White-noise background (1.10)

`Tools/white_noise.py` generates an original stationary stereo white-noise bed
without external samples. Code MIT; generated recording CC0 1.0. It is optional
and separate from pink/brown noise and the musical synthesis.
